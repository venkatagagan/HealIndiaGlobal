# Vijayawada city guide

Vijayawada is a busy commercial city on the banks of the Krishna River in Andhra Pradesh, close to the state capital region of Amaravati. It is a regional centre for healthcare in coastal Andhra Pradesh. This guide gives practical notes for patients and companions. Details marked **Check before you travel** can change, so confirm them before you rely on them.

## At a glance

| Topic | Details |
|---|---|
| State/territory | Andhra Pradesh |

| Local language(s) | Telugu. English is widely used in hospitals and hotels |
| Time zone | India Standard Time (UTC+5:30) |
| Emergency number | 112 (national emergency number) |

## Arriving by air

Vijayawada International Airport is at Gannavaram, north-east of the city. By road, allow roughly 30 to 45 minutes to central Vijayawada, longer at peak times. Many international journeys connect through a larger hub such as Hyderabad, Chennai, Bengaluru, Delhi or Mumbai, so plan for a domestic connection.

Ways to reach your hospital or accommodation:

1. **Airport pickup arranged through your coordinator or hospital.** This is the simplest option after a long journey or if you have mobility needs. Your coordinator will share the driver's details before you land.
2. **Prepaid taxis or app-based cabs.** Use the official counters or an app, and board at the marked pick-up points.

> **Check before you travel:** Check which international and domestic routes currently serve Vijayawada, and allow enough time between connecting flights, including for immigration and baggage at your first Indian airport.

## Climate by season

| Season | Months | What to expect |
|---|---|---|
| Summer | March to June | Very hot, often above 40 °C in May. Stay indoors at midday and drink plenty of fluids |
| South-west monsoon | June to September | Humid, with regular rain and occasional heavy downpours |
| North-east monsoon and cyclone season | October to December | Heavy rain and occasional cyclones along the coast; check weather warnings |
| Winter | December to February | The most comfortable months: warm days and mild evenings |

## Getting around

- **Cabs and autorickshaws** are the usual way to get around. App-based cabs and autos are widely available; agree the fare or use the meter for street autos.
- **Hospital transport** is often available for appointments; ask your coordinator.
- Traffic around the bus stand, railway station and central markets can be slow at peak times.
- **Vijayawada Junction** is one of the busiest railway stations in South India if you plan onward travel by train after your recovery.

## Recovery-friendly outings

Choose short, low-effort outings that your care team approves.

- **Prakasam Barrage.** An evening drive or short stop for views over the Krishna River.
- **Bhavani Island.** A riverside park reached by boat, with shaded areas; avoid the hottest hours.
- **Kanaka Durga Temple, Indrakeeladri hill.** An important temple overlooking the city. There are steps and crowds at festival times, so go only when you are walking comfortably, and dress modestly.

Opening days, hours, entry rules and fees change, so check before you go. Leave longer trips, such as the Undavalli caves or the Amaravati heritage sites, until later in your recovery.

## Food

Andhra food is known for being spicy. Rice with dal, pappu (lentils), sambar, rasam, pickles and curd is a typical meal, and biryani is popular. Ask for food to be made mild if you prefer. Vegetarian food is everywhere and halal food is available in many areas.

Follow the diet your care team gives you and avoid street food while you recover. The [in-country guide](../in-country-guide.md) covers food and water safety.

## Things to check before you travel

- Current flight routes into Vijayawada and connection times
- Weather and cyclone warnings from IMD, especially from October to December
- Festival dates such as Dasara at the Kanaka Durga Temple, which bring large crowds
- Dress codes and access rules at temples and other places of worship
