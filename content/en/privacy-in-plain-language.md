# Privacy in plain language

You trust us with sensitive information about your health and your travel. This page explains, in plain words, how we handle it.

> **Important:** This page is a summary. Our full Privacy Policy is the legal document that applies, and if the two differ, the Privacy Policy prevails.

## The short version

- We ask only for the information we need to arrange your care and your trip.
- Your medical information goes **only to the hospitals you choose**, and only after you have given consent.
- AI screening is optional and uses your information only if you agree.
- Sensitive details are encrypted, access is limited to people who need it, and card details stay with the payment provider.
- You can review and change your consents at any time.

## What we collect and why

| Type of information | Examples | Why we need it |
|---|---|---|
| Account | Name, email, phone number, country, preferred language. Your password is stored only in a protected, scrambled (hashed) form. | To create your account, sign you in and contact you |
| Profile and travel | Date of birth, nationality, passport details, emergency contact, companions' details | Hospital letters for visas, bookings, travel arrangements and your safety |
| Medical | Screening answers, symptoms, medical history, allergies, reports and scans you upload | To match you with hospitals and doctors, get quotes and support your care |
| Bookings and payments | Quotes, bookings, payment records, receipts. We do not store your full card number. | To process payments, refunds and receipts |
| Messages | Chats with your coordinator and hospitals, notifications | To support you and keep a record of arrangements |
| Device and security | Device type, notification token, sign-in records such as IP address and browser | To send notifications and protect your account |
| Location | Only when you choose to share it, for example when you press SOS | To help you in an urgent situation |

## Your consent, your choice

We ask for your consent at the points where it matters:

- **Terms and privacy.** These are needed to create an account.
- **AI processing.** We ask before any AI screening. You can say no and use a simpler rules-based method instead.
- **Sharing with hospitals.** We ask before your case is shared with a hospital, and you choose which hospitals receive it.
- **Marketing.** This is optional, and you can switch it off at any time.

You can see and change your consents in your profile. We keep a record of when you gave or withdrew each consent. Withdrawing a consent stops future use of your information for that purpose. It does not undo sharing that has already happened. For example, a hospital that has treated you keeps its medical records under its own legal obligations.

## Who can see your information

- **The hospitals you choose.** The hospital staff handling your case see your case details, your screening summary and the documents shared for that case. Hospitals you have not chosen do not see your medical information.
- **Our team.** Coordinators, support and finance staff see only what they need for their role.
- **Your agent.** If a registered partner agent referred you or manages your case, they can see and manage your case information.
- **Service providers you book.** Translators, drivers and accommodation providers receive only what they need, such as your name, phone number, dates and pickup point.
- **Payment providers and banks.** These receive the information needed to process your payments and refunds.
- **Our AI provider.** This applies only if you consent to AI screening, and only to produce your screening summary.
- **Technology suppliers.** These are companies that host, store or send information for us, such as email and notification services, and they act on our instructions.
- **Authorities.** We share information with them only where the law requires us to.

## How we protect your information

- **Encrypted connections** protect information travelling between your device and our servers.
- **Extra encryption** in our database protects sensitive details such as passport numbers, insurance policy numbers, bank account details and two-step verification secrets. These are shown only partly, for example the last four digits.
- **Card details** are entered on the payment provider's secure checkout and never reach our servers.
- **Limited access.** Everyone sees only what their role allows. Hospitals see only the cases shared with them, and agents see only their own patients.
- **Private documents.** Your documents are stored privately, and only people allowed to see them can download them.
- **Activity records.** We log important actions, such as sign-ins and changes to bookings, payments and refunds.
- **Account protection.** We require strong passwords and temporarily lock an account after repeated failed sign-ins. You can also turn on two-step verification.

No system is completely secure. If we become aware of a breach that affects your information, we will act quickly and tell you where the law requires.

## How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is active and for as long as we need it to provide our services. Some records, such as payment, invoice and tax records, must be kept for periods set by law. Medical records that hospitals create are kept by those hospitals under their own rules.

## Your rights

Depending on the laws that apply to you, you may be able to:

- ask for a copy of your information
- correct it
- ask us to delete it, subject to records we must keep by law
- withdraw consent
- object to marketing
- complain to a data protection authority

The full Privacy Policy explains these rights and how to use them.

## Keeping your account safe

- Use a strong password that you do not use anywhere else, and turn on two-step verification.
- Do not share your sign-in details, and sign out on shared devices.
- We will never ask for your password or one-time codes by phone, email or chat. Be wary of messages that do.

## Patients under 18

If the patient is under 18, a parent or legal guardian should create and manage the account and give consent on the child's behalf.

## Questions

If you have a question about your privacy, contact us through Messages or the Support screen in the app, or use the contact details in our full Privacy Policy.
