# Total knee replacement in Delhi NCR

Partner hospitals for knee replacement in Delhi NCR are spread across Delhi, Gurugram and Noida. The region is large, so it helps to choose accommodation close to the hospital that treats you. Daily physiotherapy visits in the first weeks after surgery are much easier when the journey is short.

## Before you travel

Your treating doctor will usually ask for recent knee X-rays, a list of your medicines and the results of routine blood tests before confirming that surgery is suitable. Your coordinator can share these with the international patient desk so that you receive a written quote before you book.

## While you are in the region

Most patients spend part of their stay in hospital and the rest recovering nearby while they build up walking with a physiotherapist. Plan for a ground-floor room or a building with a lift, and allow extra time for road travel at peak hours. The [Delhi NCR city guide](../cities/delhi-ncr.md) has notes on the airport, seasons and getting around.

> **Medical disclaimer:** This page is general information, not medical advice. Whether knee replacement is right for you, and how long you need to stay, depends on your health. Your doctor will advise.
