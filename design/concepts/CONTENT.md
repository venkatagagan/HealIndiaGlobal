# Home page content — shared by every theme

Every theme (Main, A Editorial luxury, B Clinical clarity, C Warm journey) carries exactly this information. Themes
differ in layout, photography, typography treatment, section order inside the rules below, and interaction — never in
facts, numbers, menu items or claims. The visitor picks the look they like; the information is the same.

Rules
- Use the wording below. Headline copy (H1/H2 lines, intros) may be restyled (line breaks, italics, accent words) but
  not rewritten. Small labels may be shortened only where marked "(may vary)".
- All numbers are identical across themes: 14 hospitals, 7 cities, 5,000+* (indicative), 40+ treatments, 48 hours,
  ~72 h visa, 2 to 4 weeks, CABG US $70,000 – $200,000 vs India $4,000 – $7,400.
- Section order: 1 header → 2 hero → 3 journey rail → 4 trust → … → 13 footer. Sections 5–11 may be reordered only
  if the journey numbering still reads correctly. Every section must be present.
- No review scaffolding on the page (remove any "menus shown open" / "all five menus" demo blocks). Menus open on
  hover/click and in the mobile drawer.
- English only. Emergency text exactly: "Emergency — seek local care now."
- Brand: navy #102A43, teal #0F766E (actions), cloud #F5F8FA, white, champagne #B08D57 (hairlines/accents only),
  body #334155, muted ≥ #475569, borders #E2E8F0; Manrope headings, Inter body; WCAG 2.2 AA.
