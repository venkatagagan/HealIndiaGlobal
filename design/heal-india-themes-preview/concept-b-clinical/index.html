    How it works

    Five stops. One coordinator. Door to door.

    From the first upload to the last video follow-up, every stop is planned before you pack, and one person sees it through.

    Typical time from first upload to arrival: 2 to 4 weeks

    1. 01AssessFrom home, in about 2 working days

      Tell us what is wrong, in your own words.

      Upload reports or answer a short guided questionnaire. Our AI organises your history, and a doctor on our medical team reviews it.

      • Free
      • Doctor-reviewed
      • Not a diagnosis
    2. 02ChooseMatched options within a week

      Compare matched hospitals, line by line.

      See two or three accredited options with the surgeon, room, ICU, implants and medicines priced separately. No surprises at discharge.

      • Itemised quotes
      • Doctor profiles
    3. 03TravelVisa, flights, pickup and stay

      Visa, flights and a room near the hospital.

      We prepare your e-Medical visa letter, meet you at arrivals and settle you and one companion into a stay a short drive from your hospital.

      • e-Medical visa
      • Airport pickup
      • Companion stay

    4. 04TreatIn India, with someone beside you

      Someone beside you at every appointment.

      Your coordinator and a translator join consultations, surgery day and discharge, and your family at home gets updates if you want them to.

      • Translator on site
      • WhatsApp, 24/7
    5. 05RecoverFly home, then follow-ups from home

      A plan for home, and a doctor who stays in touch.

      You fly home with discharge notes, medicines and video follow-ups booked with your surgeon. Your coordinator checks in until you are back on your feet.

      • Discharge plan
      • Video follow-ups
