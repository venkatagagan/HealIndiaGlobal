## Part 1 notes - what is not shown, and why

- Finance screens (Payments, Settlements, Accounting & reports) are only named in part 1; part 2 covers them.
- "More filters" sheets, the case "Change status" menu and the hospital "Activity" tab are not opened, to keep the
  video short. They use the same patterns shown elsewhere (filter pills, dialogs, audit trail).
- No seeded hospital or agent is approved, rejected or suspended. The approve dialogs are opened and cancelled.
  The changes shown on camera (KYC document verified, doctor registration verified, coordinator assigned, SOS
  acknowledged and resolved, support reply sent) apply only to records created for this recording:
  Kaveri Coast Orthopaedic Hospital, Savannah Health Journeys and the patient Grace Otieno.
- Translators & services has no demo requests, so that tab shows its empty state.
