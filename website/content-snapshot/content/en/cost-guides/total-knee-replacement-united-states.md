# Total knee replacement cost: India and the United States

The figures on this page are indicative ranges, not quotes. The India range is the typical range listed in our catalogue of partner hospitals; each hospital page shows its own published price. The United States range is a self-pay benchmark from the source named below the table. Your own cost depends on your health, the implant your surgeon recommends, the room you choose and how long you need to stay.

## What the India price usually covers

Hospital packages usually list what is included, such as the surgeon's fees, the operating theatre, a set number of nights in hospital and standard consumables. Items such as extra nights, additional tests, medicines to take home and your travel and accommodation are often extra. Check the inclusions and exclusions on each hospital's page and in your written quote.

## Costs that are not in the table

Remember to budget for flights, visas, accommodation for you and your companion during recovery, local transport, travel insurance and follow-up care after you return home. Your coordinator can help you list these before you decide.

> **Important:** Insurance cover, eligibility and waiting times in your home country vary. Ask your insurer and your doctor at home before you compare prices.
