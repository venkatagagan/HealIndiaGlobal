# Payments and refunds

This page explains in plain language how paying for your treatment works and what happens if you cancel.

> **Important:** This is a summary. The terms shown in your booking before you pay, including the hospital's own cancellation terms, are the ones that apply to you. If they differ from this page, your booking terms prevail. Nothing on this page affects any rights you have under applicable law.

## How your price is set

- **Quotes.** After reviewing your records, the hospital sends an itemised quote. It can include surgery, hospital stay, medicines, tests and scans, doctor fees, implants and other items, and it is valid until the date shown on it.
- **Packages.** Some hospitals offer fixed packages, such as Medical only, Medical + stay or All-inclusive. Each package lists what it includes.
- **Your booking total.** The price summary in your booking shows the subtotal, any discount, any patient service fee, any taxes, the total, the deposit, what you have paid and the balance due. A patient service fee is charged only if it is shown as a separate line before you pay.
- **Changes after you arrive.** The treating team may recommend a different plan, extra tests or a longer stay after examining you. Ask for a revised estimate before you agree to any change wherever possible. See also the [FAQ](faq.md).

## Currencies and exchange rates

- Hospitals usually price treatment in Indian rupees (INR).
- You can pay in a supported currency. These currently include the US dollar, euro, British pound, UAE dirham, Saudi riyal, Qatari riyal, Omani rial, Kuwaiti dinar, Nigerian naira, Kenyan shilling, Bangladeshi taka, Australian dollar, Canadian dollar and Indian rupee. The list may change; checkout shows the currencies available to you.
- When you start a payment, the amount is converted at an exchange rate fixed for that payment, and you see the converted amount before you confirm. If a payment request expires before you pay, a new one is created at the current rate.
- Your bank or card issuer may charge its own fees, such as for foreign transactions.

## Payment options

| Option | How it works |
|---|---|
| Pay in full | Pay the whole booking total at once. |
| Deposit | Pay a deposit to confirm your booking, then pay the balance by the due date shown in your booking. The deposit is 20% of the booking total by default; your booking shows the exact amount. |
| Instalment plan | Where it is offered for your booking, spread your payments over a set schedule, for example 3, 6 or 12 months. Each instalment has a due date and is paid in the app. Check the schedule and any conditions shown before you agree. |

Your booking is confirmed once a payment of at least the deposit amount has been received.

## Payment methods

### Card or UPI (through Razorpay)

- You pay on Razorpay's secure checkout, inside the app or web portal. Your card details are entered there and never reach our servers.
- Debit and credit cards are accepted, including many international cards. Your bank may ask you to confirm the payment, for example with a one-time password.
- UPI, India's mobile payment system, usually needs a UPI app linked to a bank account that supports it.
- Other methods, such as Indian net banking or card instalments (EMI) offered by some card issuers, may appear at checkout. Card EMI terms are set by your card issuer.
- The mobile app may ask you to confirm with your fingerprint or face before a payment.

### International bank transfer (SWIFT)

1. Choose bank transfer at checkout. The app shows the beneficiary bank details and a **unique payment reference**.
2. Send the payment from your bank, quoting the reference exactly.
3. Upload your proof of payment, such as your bank's transfer receipt or SWIFT confirmation, with the transaction reference.
4. Our finance team matches the money we receive to your booking and marks the payment as paid. You are notified when this happens.

Intermediary banks may deduct charges from international transfers. Ask your bank to send the full amount, with the charges paid by you as the sender. If less than the required amount arrives, the shortfall remains due. International transfers can take several working days, so pay well before your admission date.

> **Important:** Bank details for transfers appear only in the app or patient portal. We will never send you new bank details by email, chat or phone. If anyone asks you to pay into a different account, do not pay; contact us through the app first.

## Receipts and invoices

You can download a receipt for each payment in the app. Receipts and the hospital's itemised bill are useful for insurance claims and your own records.

## Insurance claims

If you plan to claim from your health insurance, mark "insurance claim requested" when you book. Check with your insurer before you travel, because many need approval before treatment. We can provide itemised receipts and help you get the medical documents your insurer asks for. Unless your insurer and the hospital agree otherwise in writing, you pay the hospital and claim the money back from your insurer.

## Cancellations and refunds

### Default refund tiers

Unless your hospital's or booking terms say otherwise, this default policy applies when you cancel a booking before your admission date:

| When you cancel | Refund of the amount you have paid towards the booking |
|---|---|
| 14 days or more before your admission date | 100% |
| 7 to 13 days before your admission date | 50% |
| Less than 7 days before your admission date | No refund |

- The number of days is counted from the day you cancel to your scheduled admission date.
- Some hospitals and packages have different terms. These are shown in your booking before you pay, and they prevail over the default policy.
- Services already provided, such as a completed video consultation, may not be refundable. Check the terms shown when you booked.
- Money you paid directly to third parties, such as visa fees, airlines or hotels you booked yourself, follows those providers' own rules.
- Once you have been admitted, a booking can no longer be cancelled in the app. Speak to your coordinator about any questions on charges.

### How to cancel

1. **Talk to your coordinator first.** The hospital may be able to move your dates instead.
2. **Cancel in the app.** Open your booking, choose Cancel and give a reason. The refundable amount is worked out under the terms that apply to your booking.
3. **Follow the refund.** A refund moves through these stages: requested, approved, processing and processed. You can see its status in the app.

### Where refunds go

- Card and UPI payments are refunded to the original card or account.
- Bank transfers are refunded to the account they came from. We may ask you to confirm the account details through the app.

### How long refunds take

Once a refund is processed, how long it takes to reach you depends on your bank and the payment provider. If a refund shows as processed but you have not received it, contact support with your booking number.

### Exchange rates and refunds

Refunds are calculated in the currency of your booking, usually Indian rupees, and converted back. The amount you receive in your own currency may differ from what you paid because of exchange-rate changes and bank charges, which are outside our control.

### If you cannot travel for reasons outside your control

If your visa is refused, or a doctor advises you not to travel, contact your coordinator straight away. The hospital may be able to reschedule. Any exception to the refund terms is decided under the hospital's and booking terms, and we will help you through the process.

### If the final bill differs from what you paid

If your final bill is higher than what you have paid, the hospital or your coordinator will tell you how to pay the difference. If you have paid more than your final bill, ask your coordinator how the difference will be handled; it is dealt with under your booking terms.

## Failed or duplicate payments

- If a payment fails, try again or choose another method.
- If money left your account but the payment shows as failed, it is usually returned automatically by your bank or the payment provider. If it is not returned, contact support with your payment order number, which starts with "HIG-P".
- If you think you paid twice, contact support. Duplicate payments are refunded to the original payment method.

## Questions and disputes

If you have a question or disagree with a charge, contact your coordinator or support through the app first, and we will look into it. This does not affect any rights you have with your card issuer or under applicable law.
