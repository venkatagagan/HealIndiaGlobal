## 1. Definitions

- **"Agent Code"** and **"Referral Link"** mean the unique code (for example `AGT7K2QX`) and link issued to you in
  the agent portal.
- **"Attributed Booking"** means a Booking for a Case that is linked to you on the Platform at Booking Completion,
  because (a) you created the Case for a Patient you manage on the Platform, or (b) the Patient registered using
  your Agent Code or Referral Link and created the Case within [[12 months]] of registering.
- **"Booking"**, **"Booking Completion"**, **"Case"** and **"Hospital"** have the meanings used on the Platform: a
  booking for treatment, its completion on the Patient's discharge, a Patient's enquiry to a hospital, and a
  hospital listed on the Platform.
- **"Commission Base"** or **"H"** means, for a Booking, the amounts paid by or for the Patient through the Platform
  for hospital services, less refunds and any patient service fee, in INR (the same base used for the Hospital's
  settlement).
- **"Patient"** means an individual seeking treatment through the Platform and, where relevant, their parent or
  guardian.
- **"Patient Authorisation"** means a written authorisation from a Patient in the form of Schedule 2 (or a form
  with equivalent content).
- **"Patient Data"** means personal data of Patients and their companions.
- **"Platform"** means the Heal India Global websites, apps, portals and APIs; **"Portal"** means the agent portal.

## 4. How you deal with Patients

4.1 **No medical advice.** You must not diagnose, recommend or advise against any treatment, interpret test results,
or present screening results (AI or rules-based) as a diagnosis. You may share the information shown on the
Platform and must refer clinical questions to Hospital doctors.

4.2 **Honest information.** You must describe costs, outcomes, accreditations, visas and timelines accurately and
only as shown on the Platform. In particular:

- (a) quotes are estimates until a doctor examines the Patient in person;
- (b) no outcome, visa or timeline can be guaranteed;
- (c) accreditations belong to individual Hospitals, not to us; and
- (d) neither you nor we are part of, or approved by, any government.

You must not use government emblems or names of government programmes, or describe yourself as an employee or
official representative of us or of any Hospital. You may describe yourself as "an independent facilitator
registered on Heal India Global".

4.3 **Disclosure.** Before a Patient shares information with you for the Platform or books anything, you must tell
them in writing that:

- (a) you are an independent facilitator;
- (b) you receive a commission from the Platform Company on completed bookings;
- (c) what fees, if any, you will charge them directly; and
- (d) they may contact the Platform Company directly at any time.

4.4 **Your own fees.** You may charge a Patient a fee for your own services only if you disclose it in writing
beforehand, the Patient agrees in writing, and you do not present it as a Hospital or Platform charge. You must not
mark up Hospital quotes or Platform prices.

4.5 **Patient money.**

- (a) Payments for treatment must be made through the Platform's checkout. You must not collect payment for
  treatment into your own accounts.
- (b) You may pay through the Platform on a Patient's behalf only with the Patient's authorisation, and you must
  give the Patient a receipt.
- (c) Refunds of payments you made are returned to your payment method. You must pass each such refund to the
  Patient within [[7]] days and show us evidence on request.

4.6 **Patient Authorisation and notices.** Before you create a Patient profile, upload documents, run a screening,
share a Case with a Hospital, accept a quote, book, pay or cancel for a Patient, you must:

- (a) obtain a signed Patient Authorisation (from a parent or guardian for a Patient under 18);
- (b) give the Patient our Privacy Policy, AI Screening Consent and Data Sharing Consent in a language they
  understand;
- (c) provide the Patient's email address where possible, so that we can invite them to their own account;
- (d) keep the Patient Authorisation for [[3]] years after the last Booking; and
- (e) produce it within [[2]] business days of our request.

When you confirm a consent in the Portal on a Patient's behalf, you confirm that you have done all of these things.

4.7 **Fair dealing.** You must present options fairly, must not steer Patients because of differences in commission,
must not pressure Patients, and must not discourage them from contacting us or a Hospital directly.

4.8 **Emergencies.** If a Patient appears to have urgent symptoms, tell them to seek immediate local emergency care
and do not arrange travel.

4.9 **Prohibited activities.** You must not, directly or indirectly:

- (a) source, introduce or arrange organ or tissue donors, or facilitate any commercial dealing in human organs
  (Transplantation of Human Organs and Tissues Act, 1994);
- (b) facilitate sex determination or sex selection, commercial surrogacy, or any treatment unlawful in India;
- (c) provide false documents, or help anyone obtain a medical or medical-attendant visa for a non-medical purpose
  or for a person who is not a genuine attendant;
- (d) take part in human trafficking, insurance fraud or money laundering;
- (e) post fake reviews or testimonials;
- (f) send spam or unsolicited marketing in breach of the law of the recipient's country; or
- (g) impersonate any person.

4.10 **Local law.** You will comply with the laws of every country in which you operate, including licensing,
advertising, consumer protection, data protection and anti-spam laws.

## 5. Commission

5.1 **Entitlement.** We pay you commission on each Attributed Booking at your Agent Commission Rate (Schedule 1)
multiplied by the Commission Base (H). Commission is paid by us out of our own commission from the Hospital. It is
never charged to Patients or Hospitals, and your commission for a Booking will not exceed our commission for it.

5.2 **When earned.** Commission is earned only at Booking Completion and only on amounts actually received and not
refunded. Amounts received after Booking Completion (for example installments) earn commission when received. No
commission is earned on cancelled Bookings [[or on Cancellation Retention — business decision]], teleconsultation
fees, or travel and other services.

5.3 **Attribution.** Each Case is attributed to at most one agent. Attribution follows the Platform's records. If
two agents claim the same Patient, the earlier valid link on the Platform prevails. We will resolve disputes
reasonably on the evidence, and our decision is final unless clearly wrong. There is no commission where you, your
personnel or [[your immediate family]] are the Patient.

5.4 **Settlement.** Commission becomes eligible for payout after the hold period (currently 3 days after Booking
Completion) and is paid in the next settlement batch for your payee cycle (currently every 7 days), once the batch
has been created by one of our authorised finance users and approved by a different one. You can see items,
statements and payouts in the Portal.

5.5 **Payout.** We pay to a verified bank account in your legal name: in INR for accounts in India [[through a
Razorpay Route linked account or bank transfer]], and by international bank transfer for accounts outside India.
For international transfers, receiving and intermediary bank charges and currency conversion by your bank are your
responsibility. Payouts are suspended while your KYC or bank details are unverified.

5.6 **Holds.** We may hold commission, telling you why in the Portal, where there is a Patient complaint or dispute,
a chargeback, a reasonable suspicion of fraud or breach, incomplete KYC, a missing Patient Authorisation, or a legal
or regulatory order.

5.7 **Clawbacks.** If a Patient is refunded after Booking Completion, your commission on that Booking is reduced in
the same proportion and a negative item is set off against your next commission. Any negative balance outstanding
for more than [[30]] days is payable within [[15]] days of our invoice. We may set off any amount you owe us against
amounts we owe you.

5.8 **Taxes.**

- (a) We deduct or withhold tax as the law requires: for agents resident in India, tax at source on commission
  under section 194H of the Income-tax Act, 1961 or the corresponding provision of the Income-tax Act, 2025
  [[currently 2% above the annual threshold — verify]]; for non-resident agents, withholding under the
  corresponding provision for payments to non-residents, subject to any tax treaty benefit for which you provide
  the required documents (for example a tax residency certificate, Form 10F or its successor, and a no-permanent-
  establishment declaration).
- (b) If you are registered for GST in India, you must issue a valid GST tax invoice for your commission and we
  will pay the GST shown, subject to it being reported in your returns.
- (c) If you are outside India, we may account for Indian GST under the reverse-charge mechanism where the law
  requires; this will not reduce your commission.
- (d) You are responsible for all other taxes on your income.

> After the omission of section 13(8)(b) of the IGST Act by the Finance Act, 2026, commission paid to foreign

5.9 **No other payments.** You must not accept any commission, fee or benefit from a Hospital, accommodation
provider, translator, transport provider or other provider in connection with a Patient you introduced through the
Platform.

5.10 **Changes.** We may change your Agent Commission Rate or tier on [[30]] days' notice in the Portal. Changes do
not affect Bookings confirmed before they take effect. If you do not accept a change, you may terminate under
clause 11.2.

## 6. Data protection

6.1 **Roles.** You are an independent Data Fiduciary for personal data you collect in your own business. When you use
the Portal, you access Patient Data held by us only as an authorised user, for the purpose of helping that Patient
with their Case (the "**Permitted Purpose**"). We are the Data Fiduciary for the Platform's records.

6.2 **Lawful basis.** You confirm that you have the Patient's consent and authority, and that you have given the
notices required by clause 4.6, before you enter or use their data on the Platform.

6.3 **Use limits.** You must not use Patient Data for any other purpose, market to Patients without their separate
consent, sell or share Patient Data (except with Hospitals and providers through the Platform or as the Patient
instructs), or copy, export or scrape data from the Portal in bulk.

6.4 **Security.** You must:

- (a) protect the devices you use for the Portal (screen lock, supported and updated software, encryption where
  available);
- (b) keep your credentials secret and use two-step verification when it is offered;
- (c) not store Patient documents in personal or unsecured locations; and
- (d) delete local copies of Patient Data within [[30]] days after the Case is closed, unless the law requires you
  to keep them.

6.5 **Breaches.** You must tell us within 24 hours if you become aware of any unauthorised access to, loss or
disclosure of Patient Data, or any compromise of your Portal account, and cooperate with us.

6.6 **Requests.** You must forward to us, within [[3]] business days, any request or complaint from a Patient about
data held on the Platform.

6.7 **Your country's law.** You will comply with the data protection law of the countries where you operate and will
not transfer Patient Data onwards except as the Patient's Case requires.

6.8 **On termination.** Your Portal access ends. You must return or delete Patient Data obtained through the Platform,
except where the law requires you to keep it, and confirm deletion on request.

## 7. Anti-bribery, anti-kickback, sanctions and anti-money laundering

7.1 You will comply with the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988 and with the anti-corruption laws that apply to you,
including, where applicable, the US Foreign Corrupt Practices Act and the UK Bribery Act 2010.

7.2 You will not offer, give, request or accept anything of value, directly or through others, to or from:

- (a) any public official — including embassy, consular, immigration and customs staff, and officials of ministries
  of health or of government or employer patient-sponsorship programmes;
- (b) any doctor or healthcare worker, in India or abroad, in return for a referral or recommendation;
- (c) any Hospital's personnel;
- (d) any insurer's personnel or any employer's staff responsible for medical benefits; or
- (e) our personnel,

in order to obtain or reward a referral, a visa, a decision or any other advantage. Facilitation ("speed")
payments are prohibited.

7.3 Gifts and hospitality must be modest, infrequent, transparent and recorded, and must never be used to influence
a medical decision, a review or a referral.

7.4 You will keep accurate records of payments connected with Patients you introduce, allow us to inspect them on
reasonable notice, and confirm your compliance with this clause in writing once a year.

7.5 You confirm that neither you nor your owners or key personnel are subject to sanctions administered by the
United Nations, India or any other authority that applies to you, and you will not use the Platform for the benefit
of a sanctioned person or territory. We may refuse or delay a payout where the law requires.

7.6 You will not split, disguise or route payments through third parties to avoid controls. You must tell us
promptly about any suspicious transaction.

7.7 A breach of this clause allows us to terminate immediately, withhold commission on affected Bookings, and claim
the losses it causes us.

## 10. Liability and indemnity

10.1 You will indemnify us against losses, liabilities, penalties and reasonable legal costs arising from your
misrepresentation to Patients, processing of Patient Data without authority, breach of clauses 4, 6 or 7, breach of
law, or failure to pay your own taxes.

10.2 Neither party is liable to the other for indirect or consequential loss, or loss of profit or goodwill.

10.3 Our total liability to you in any 12-month period is limited to the commission paid or payable to you in the
preceding 12 months.

10.4 Clauses 10.2 and 10.3 do not limit liability for fraud or wilful misconduct, your indemnity under clause 10.1, or
either party's obligation to pay amounts due.

## 11. Term, suspension and termination

11.1 **Term.** This Agreement starts when we activate your account and continues until terminated.

11.2 **Termination for convenience.** Either party may terminate on [[30]] days' notice through the Portal or by email.

11.3 **Termination for cause.** We may suspend your account immediately, and may terminate this Agreement with
immediate effect, if:

- (a) you breach clause 4, 6 or 7;
- (b) you commit fraud or misrepresent yourself;
- (c) your KYC fails or lapses;
- (d) you become subject to sanctions;
- (e) a regulator or court requires it;
- (f) we receive repeated substantiated Patient complaints about you; or
- (g) you commit any other material breach not remedied within [[15]] days of notice.

11.4 **Effect of termination.**

- (a) Commission on Attributed Bookings completed before termination is paid under clause 5, subject to holds and
  clawbacks.
- (b) Commission on Bookings completed within [[90]] days after termination, for Cases created before termination,
  is also paid — unless we terminated under clause 11.3, in which case commission on Bookings affected by the
  breach is forfeited.
- (c) Patients you introduced remain users of the Platform. We will unlink your access to their accounts and tell
  them, and they may continue directly with us or with another agent.
- (d) Clauses 5.6, 5.7, 6.8, 7, 10, 12 and 13 survive termination.

## 12. Confidentiality

You will keep confidential all non-public information about the Platform, our commercial terms, Hospitals and
Patients, and use it only for this Agreement, during the term and for [[3]] years afterwards (indefinitely for
Patient Data).

13.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of India.

13.2 The parties will first try to resolve any dispute in good faith within 30 days of a written notice of dispute.

13.3 Unresolved disputes will be finally resolved by arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, by

agents outside India, the arbitration is an international commercial arbitration seated in [[CITY]]. The courts at
[[CITY]] have exclusive jurisdiction for interim relief and matters relating to the arbitration.

14.4 **Entire agreement and severability.** This Agreement and its Schedules are the entire agreement on its subject.
